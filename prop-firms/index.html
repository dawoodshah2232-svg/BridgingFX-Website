The stack

Purpose-built for the prop model.

Generic brokerage tools duct-taped together is why most prop firms stall. This stack was designed for evaluations at scale.

Challenge & evaluation engines

1-step, 2-step, and instant-funding models with tick-level rule enforcement — profit targets, drawdown limits, consistency rules, and minimum trading days, all bulletproof.

Real-time risk monitoring

Drawdown breaches, HFT and latency-arbitrage detection, news-trading violations, and cross-account copy patterns — flagged automatically, reviewed by humans.

Trader dashboards

Progress tracking traders actually trust: targets, drawdown buffers, payout history, and scaling plans in a dashboard worth screenshotting — your best marketing asset.

04

Scaling & payouts

Scaling plans, profit splits, and payout scheduling fully automated with audit trails. Payouts arrive on time, every time — the foundation of prop-firm reputation.

Anti-cheat infrastructure

Prop websites & funnels

Why it matters

In prop, infrastructure is reputation.

A single miscalculated drawdown breach or one delayed payout — and social media does the rest. The prop firms winning 2026 share one trait: evaluation infrastructure their traders trust completely.
